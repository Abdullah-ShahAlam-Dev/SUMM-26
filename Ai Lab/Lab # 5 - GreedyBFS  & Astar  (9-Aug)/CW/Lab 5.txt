Main aapko dono ka farq asaan ilfaaz mein bata deta hoon:

1. Breadth-First Search (BFS)

Type: Ye ek "Uninformed Search" (blind search) hai.

Working: Ye level-by-level kaam karta hai. Pehle root node ke saare padosi check karega, phir unke padosi check karega, aur aese hi aage barhega.

Data Structure: Isme simple Queue (First-In-First-Out) use hoti hai.

Logic: Is algorithm ko pehle se koi idea nahi hota ke goal kahan hai, ye bas andha-dhun sab check karta hai.

2. Best-First Search (Greedy Best-First Search - GBFS)

Type: Ye ek "Informed Search" (heuristic search) hai.

Working: Ye level-by-level nahi chalta. Ye hamesha us padosi (neighbor) par jump karta hai jiski Heuristic value sab se kam hoti hai (yani jo goal ke sab se qareeb lagta hai).

Data Structure: Isme Priority Queue use hoti hai (jaisa ke aapne apne class task mein import heapq likha tha).

Logic: Isme algorithm ko goal ki distance ka ek estimate (heuristic values) pehle se diya jata hai.

Naam padhne mein thore same lagte hain, lekin inke kaam karne ka tarika zameen asmaan ka farq rakhta hai:

Breadth-First Search (BFS): Ye bilkul "andha" (blind) search hai. Isko nahi pata manzil kahan hai, ye bas har taraf ek ek kar ke sab kuch check karta hai.

Best-First Search (GBFS): Ye "smart" (informed) search hai. Iske paas ek andaza (heuristic) hota hai, toh ye fizool raaste chhor kar direct us raaste par jata hai jo goal ke sab se qareeb lagta hai.
